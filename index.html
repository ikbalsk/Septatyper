Method

A septatype is 7 values joined by dashes: the amino acid at GyrA-83, GyrA-87, ParC-80, ParC-84 and RecN-346, then qnrB present (1) or absent (0), then qnrS present (1) or absent (0). Example: L-N-I-E-L-0-0.

For each species and isolation site, 2,734 reference isolates are grouped by septatype. The majority phenotype is the call: Resistant if resistant isolates outnumber susceptible ones, otherwise Susceptible (ties go to Susceptible). A septatype never seen for that species and site returns Unknown; no call is forced.

From sequence: BLAST+ finds gyrA, parC, recN and qnr genes against verified NCBI references; codons at the 5 positions are read from the alignment; qnr counts as present at 80% identity and 90% coverage.

Limits

  • Accuracy is measured on the same isolates that define the rules; not independent validation.
  • Statistical prediction, not a substitute for laboratory testing.
  • Research use only.
